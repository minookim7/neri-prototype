    Read the foundation for parents

    Before anything else was made, God spoke, and light came. Light is the first gift of creation: it lets us see everything else He made. Genesis 1 shows a God who brings order out of darkness and calls what He makes good.

    For a baby or toddler, this is learned through the body before it is understood in words: the warmth of morning sun, the comfort of a lamp at night, the delight of a torch in a dark room. When you name these moments (“God made light”), you are laying down the first, simplest truth about who God is: the Maker, who is good.

    Later in Scripture, Jesus says, “I am the light of the world” (John 8:12). You don’t need to explain that yet. It is where this story is going.

    Draft. Before launch, every foundation will be reviewed by someone trained in theology and early education.
